Before You Flash
    
                        
  • Connect the C5 radio board to USB.
    • 
                        
  • Use Chrome or Edge with a USB data cable.
    • 
                        
  • Hold BOOT, press and release RESET, then release BOOT before connecting.
    • 
                        
  • Close serial monitors using this USB port.
    • 
                    

                    
Choose Your Mode
Single Pilot · SPI
FPVGateC5RX replaces an RX5808. A resistor/capacitor filter provides analog RSSI to your timer.
Multi Pilot · UART
FPVGateC5MK measures up to eight pilots over a two-wire UART link. Requires C5 multi-pilot support on your FPVGate timer.

                    
Alpha Firmware
Early firmware for testing. Validate reception and lap detection with your hardware before racing.

Flash Complete


                                
                                

                                    Unplug your FPVGate, then plug it back in.
                                    

                                        This step is required. After flashing, the board waits in its
                                        bootloader and will not start the new firmware until power has
                                        been removed. Pressing reset is not enough — the cable has
                                        to come out.
                                    

                                


                                

                                    Once it is back, prepare your SD card using the tool in the sidebar,
                                    then open the interface below.
                                


                                

                                
                                

                                    
If that did not open, try one of these directly:

                                    
    
                                        
  • 192.168.7.1 — over the USB cable
    • 
                                        
  • 192.168.4.1 — when connected to the FPVGate WiFi network
    • 
                                        
  • fpvgate.local — when it has joined your own WiFi
    • 
                                    

                                


                                
                                

                                    Not showing up over USB?
                                    

                                        Unplug and replug it once more. Windows sometimes brings the USB
                                        network adapter back disabled the first time, and a second power
                                        cycle clears it. Your FPVGate is also reachable over WiFi in the
                                        meantime.

Quick Tips

                        
    
                            
  • Connect your ESP32 via USB before starting
    • 
                            
  • First time? Enable "Full Chip Erase"
    • 
                            
  • Hold BOOT button if device isn't detected
    • 
                            
  • Flashing takes 2-3 minutes
