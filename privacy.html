Privacy policy

How FPVGate handles personal information.

Who to contact

FPVGate is responsible for personal information used by this website, store and support service. For privacy questions, contact shop.fpvgate@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Orders: contact details, delivery and billing information, products, prices, payment references, shipping and order history.
  • Support and distributor enquiries: the details you submit, order references, messages, internal support notes, responses and delivery records.
  • Website usage: events such as page views, product selections and flash outcomes; browser information, referrer, country, time and an IP-derived hash. See the data collection notice.

Stripe handles checkout payment details. The FPVGate order system stores payment references rather than full card details.

Why we use it

We use information to process orders, take payment, arrange delivery, investigate faults, provide support, discuss distributor applications and keep financial and operational records. Website events help us understand use and diagnose flasher problems.

We process information to fulfil orders and provide the support or business discussions you request. We rely on legal obligations for records we are required to keep, and legitimate interests for service security and understanding service performance. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests, including optional analytics, by contacting us or using the analytics preference controls.

Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Services involved

The site uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Cloudflare for APIs and storage, Stripe for payments, Shippo and delivery carriers for shipping, and Resend for email delivery. Enquiries are also sent to the FPVGate Gmail inbox. If staff Discord alerts are configured, the alert contains a ticket reference, category and private admin link, not the customer message or contact details. Public pages may load fonts, libraries or files from services including Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg and GitHub.

These services receive information needed for their role and may process it outside your country. Their own privacy terms describe their processing and applicable protections. Contact us if you need details of a particular service or transfer.

Retention and access

Public enquiry forms also keep an IP-derived hash and submission counters to limit abuse. Expired counters are removed during later submissions; the original IP address is not written to that counter table.

Support/enquiry content, routine email-delivery logs and potentially identifiable website analytics are retained in our application databases for up to 365 days, with scheduled daily cleanup. Anonymous monthly counts containing no contact information, IP hashes, URLs or message text may be retained indefinitely. Admin access is restricted to authorised accounts.

Financial and order records that must be retained for tax, accounting or other legal obligations are an exception and may be kept for the legally required period. Messages in our email inbox, provider records and backups are managed separately under their applicable retention arrangements; contact us about any copies relating to your enquiry.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your information, and may object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. Some records may still need to be kept to meet legal obligations.

Send a request to shop.fpvgate@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing personal information. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.
