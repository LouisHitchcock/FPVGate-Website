How can we help?

Use this form for an order query, delivery issue, cancellation or faulty device. Your request is saved as a private support ticket. We reply by email; there is no public ticket-history lookup.

For a faulty device, tell us the model, symptoms and troubleshooting already attempted. We will discuss the next step before arranging a return.

Please do not include passwords or payment-card details. The order reference helps us investigate; it does not give access to order details.

Prefer email?

Contact shop.fpvgate@gmail.com. If you already have a ticket reference, include it in your email. Incoming email replies reach our inbox; they are not automatically added to the online ticket history.

For wholesale enquiries, please use the distributor page.
