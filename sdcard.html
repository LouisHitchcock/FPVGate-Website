Requirements

  • MicroSD card (any size, 1GB+ recommended)
  • SD card formatted as FAT32
  • Chrome, Edge, or Opera browser
  • SD card reader connected to your computer

What Gets Installed

  • Voice announcement audio files
  • Multiple voice options
  • Correct folder structure
  • Config templates (if included)

Alternative Method

You can also manually download the SD_Card.zip from the GitHub releases and extract it to your SD card.
