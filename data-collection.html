Website and flasher analytics

On pages that include the analytics tracker, FPVGate records usage events such as page views, board and version choices, flash starts, successes and errors, product views and cart activity. Events may include product identifiers and prices, browser information, referrer, approximate country and a timestamp.

The analytics API receives your IP address through its hosting provider and stores a shortened hash for estimating distinct visitors. A hash is pseudonymous information; it should not be treated as fully anonymous. Hosting and other service providers may separately process network information.

The tracker sends events automatically unless you disable it below. We use this information to understand service performance, not for advertising. Identifiable event details are retained for up to 365 days with daily cleanup; only anonymous monthly counts may be kept indefinitely. You can disable future optional tracker events in this browser below.

Your choice is saved in this browser. It applies to future events from the FPVGate tracker, not to required order processing, support submissions, hosting logs or other providers. It does not delete previously recorded events.

Orders and support are separate

Checkout and support forms need the details you provide to process an order or respond to you. The support form saves a private ticket and sends a notification to the FPVGate team. Public ticket references do not provide access to ticket contents or order information.

Read the privacy policy for the services involved and how to contact us about your data.
