# FPVGate v1.9.0

FPVGate 1.9.0 adds multi-pilot timing with the ESP32-C5 receiver, gate-managed heats and the Event Director.

## Multi-pilot timing and race day

- Time up to eight pilots on one ESP32-C5, including Raceband R8. Set channels, names, colours, thresholds and calibration per pilot, and follow live standings, lap tables and race statistics.
- C5 heats can finish on max laps or heat time and save on the gate with every browser closed. Conventional receiver workflows still use the race browser for browser-managed automation and announcements.
- Enable Event Mode in Settings → User Interface. Set up pilots, groups, rules and rounds with a guided wizard or the full-page editor. Guided, Club and Custom presets include schedule and single-heat layouts.
- The gate persists Event results and can prepare the next heat without starting it. Optional result review blocks another start until confirmed. Results retain pilot identity after corrections, skipped heats remain marked, and the gate can reconcile a saved result after a restart.
- ELRS Backpack race control is a beta, disabled by default. Configure the TX backpack binding phrase and DVR Rec switch for race start, countdown and stop. C5 crossings also drive the gate LEDs.

## History, race analysis and audio

- Record all eight RSSI channels to SD and inspect individual traces in Visual Marshal. Edit, remove or merge laps per pilot. Race analysis includes fastest lap, fastest three consecutive laps, best three and median.
- Configuration and Race History have revised layouts. History supports filtering, title sorting, access to older races and more robust imports with preserved timestamps.
- Browser announcements support consecutive laps, Beep Every Lap, Say Lap Time Too, selectable crossing beeps with previews and downloadable voice packs.
- Bulk history import and clear yield during SD work to avoid the watchdog resets seen with large multi-pilot histories. Storage listings continue past unreadable entries. SD startup includes low-speed retry.

## Receivers, connectivity and diagnostics

- RotorHazard plugin protocol 2 supports passes from every pilot, full-resolution live RSSI at 20 Hz and pass peaks for timing and Marshal. The separately maintained [RotorHazard plugin](https://github.com/LouisHitchcock/fpvgate-rh-plugin/releases) provides its own compatibility and installation notes.
- Update an online, compatible C5 receiver in Settings → System with its matching image. The gate observes the trial boot before declaring success. The standard C5MK AIO uses C5 GPIO11 TX / GPIO12 RX (XIAO D6/D7) and the 'c5mkaio' image. Legacy D4/D5 wiring uses 'xiaoc5'.
- Waveshare ESP32-S3-ETH supports Ethernet DHCP and the direct-link fallback at 192.168.8.1. The Wi-Fi access point uses 20 MHz channels. Web asset transfers and PSRAM use were revised to reduce internal-memory pressure during concurrent loads.
- Diagnostics include C5 receiver checks, a buzzer test, memory and connection details, downloadable crash-retained logs, optional SD log saving and core dumps. The interface includes English and Chinese localisation.

## Installing

Use the [FPVGate flasher](https://fpvgate.xyz/flasher.html) and select your exact board. This package contains **FPVGate C5MK AIO, FPVGate Novacore AIO, Seeed XIAO ESP32S3, Waveshare ESP32-S3-ETH and ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1**. Solo and XIAO ESP32S3 Plus retain their existing 1.8.x releases.

Back up settings, Event data and race history. From compatible 1.8.x or 1.9.0 alpha builds, update both app and filesystem for your board. From 1.7.x, use a full USB flash for the changed partition table. The S3 and C5 have separate images. Pair the AIO with [C5MK v1.3.0](https://github.com/LouisHitchcock/FPVGateC5MK/releases/tag/v1.3.0).

## Known limitations

- One AIO lost its C5 link after an S3-only cold start. A manual C5 BOOT/reset sequence restored RSSI on that unit. Automatic recovery and the cause remain unverified.
- Independent eight-transmitter RF and complete physical ELRS switch qualification remain open. The combined race, page-load and live-stream test on one AIO reached 39,212 bytes of free internal heap against a 40,000-byte acceptance floor.
- ELRS goggle OSD, direct FPVScores integration, experimental C5MK 2.0 timing and in-gate SD-card flashing are deferred.
