Delivery options and costs

Available delivery services and charges are shown at checkout after you enter your delivery details. If no suitable option appears, contact us before ordering.

Dispatch and delivery

In-stock orders normally dispatch within 3–5 business days. Dispatch time is separate from the carrier's delivery estimate. Estimates are not guaranteed arrival dates.

If there is an issue with your order, contact us with your order reference so we can investigate.

Pre-orders

A pre-order product displays its expected ships-from date. Orders containing pre-order items ship together once the required stock is available, as explained at checkout. Contact us if you need to discuss an existing pre-order.

Tracking and delivery problems

Where tracking is available, the dispatch email includes the tracking information. For missing parcels, damaged deliveries or an incorrect address, open a support ticket. Please keep packaging if the parcel arrived damaged.

Reporting a problem helps us investigate with the carrier; it does not remove your rights as a customer.

International orders

Delivery availability depends on the order and the options offered at checkout. Customs processing can affect delivery times. Import duties, taxes or carrier fees may apply; contact us before ordering if you need clarification about these charges.

Need help?

Contact shop.fpvgate@gmail.com or open an order-support ticket. Please include your order reference and the email used at checkout.
