If your device appears faulty

  1. Open a support ticket with your order reference, device model and a description of the fault.
  2. We will work through troubleshooting with you. Many setup or firmware issues can be resolved without returning hardware.
  3. If the fault cannot be resolved through support, we will arrange the return and cover the agreed return shipping. We will provide a prepaid label where available, or agree another paid return arrangement with you.
  4. After we receive the faulty device, we will arrange a replacement with you.

Please wait for the return instructions before sending a device. Return details are provided privately through support.

This is our faulty-device support process. It does not replace your statutory rights to an appropriate refund, repair or replacement.

What to include in your ticket

Include your order reference, the email used to buy the device, the model, what happened and any troubleshooting already attempted. You do not need to send passwords or payment-card details.

Cancelling a consumer online order

Where UK consumer cancellation rights apply, you can normally notify us within 14 days of delivery that you wish to cancel, then return the goods within a further 14 days. You do not have to give a reason.

Eligible refunds include standard outbound delivery, but not the extra cost of a premium service. Refunds are normally made within 14 days of receiving the goods back or evidence of return, as applicable. Handling beyond what is needed to inspect the goods may affect the refund.

Tell us you wish to cancel, or email shop.fpvgate@gmail.com. Include your name, order reference, goods to cancel and delivery date. Asking for return instructions does not require you to wait for our permission to exercise a statutory cancellation right.

For a non-faulty cancellation return, you pay the return postage. This is separate from faulty-device returns, where FPVGate covers the agreed return shipping.

Other purchases and your rights

Business and distributor orders use the commercial terms agreed with the buyer. Consumer rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law. Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Read the GOV.UK returns and refunds guidance for more information.
