Personal FPV Lap Timer

Compact, self-contained RSSI-based lap timing for 5.8GHz FPV drones

No transponders, no complex setup - just plug in, calibrate, and fly. Perfect for personal practice sessions and small indoor tracks.

Flash Firmware Documentation View on GitHub

Key Features

Everything the timer does on its own, without a laptop, a transponder or a race director.

Visual Feedback

RGB LED indicators with 10 customizable presets and real-time RSSI visualization

Voice Announcements

Natural voice announcements with 4 included voices and phonetic name support

Multilingual Voice Packs

Lap calls in English, German, Spanish or French, with alternate voices you can swap on the SD card

Race Analysis

Real-time lap tracking, lap comparison charts, consistency analysis, and race history

Race Notes

Jot notes during or after a race, saved with the run so you can see what you changed and why

Track Management

Create and manage track profiles with distance tracking and custom metadata

Multi-Pilot Racing

Sync multiple timers for group races with side-by-side pilot comparison

RotorHazard Integration

Bidirectional race control with RotorHazard, NTP clock sync, and automatic lap submission

Supported Bands

Analog and digital video systems are both detected on the same hardware.

Analog FPV

  • Boscam A, B, E
  • Fatshark
  • RaceBand
  • LowBand

Digital FPV

  • DJI (v1-25, v1-50, 03/04 variants)
  • HDZero (R, E, F, CE)
  • WalkSnail (R, 25, 50)
